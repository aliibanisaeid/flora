# Flora design direction

## Adapted website-builder brief

Build FLORA, a bilingual Persian/English flower shop. Combine the continuous cinematic scroll experience of https://loomere.higgsfield.app/ with the editorial flower collections and occasion browsing of https://woshe.co/

Start in Persian RTL and use Bonyade Koodak for every Persian interface element. English uses Cabinet Grotesk headings and Inter Tight body text. Use warm ivory, deep forest green, burgundy actions and subtle bronze rules. Add an original botanical emblem and elegant uppercase Flora wordmark

One continuous 15-second film follows a peony bud as it opens, gathers with other flowers and becomes an arrangement. Let scrolling seek the film both forward and backward across six chapters: stem, bud, petals, arrangement, bouquet and color choices. The final chapter shows four palettes around a forest-green panel. Keep an accessible hero scroll arrow, but omit the visible scroll instruction, small fact badges and collection-explore links from the film

Use a stable header with Collections, Occasions, Bespoke and The Studio. Add generous editorial photography, four demonstration products, collection filters, detail dialogs, a live color/size/wrapping composer, persistent bag, quantity controls, care accordions and an order preview. Use the Flora botanical emblem for editorial action details instead of arrows. All care answers start closed, cards and controls have rounded corners, and public titles have no terminal periods

## Message

Persian: «برای آنچه می‌خواهید بگویید»

English: “For everything you want to say”

The supporting copy explains bouquets, boxes and vase arrangements. Sections move from the emotional introduction to specific choices. The Persian voice uses «شما» consistently. Copy research and alternatives are documented in messaging-research.md

## Motion and mobile

A Blob-backed video seek queue coalesces rapid scroll changes. Posters are exact frames from each device's encoded film. Reduced motion uses six static chapter panels and does not request the film. Mobile uses a center-cropped 9:16 film, two product columns, a stacked composer and horizontal occasion cards

## Output

The editable project lives in Downloads/Flowershop. Higgsfield supplied creative guidance, original flower photography, the continuous film and the original logo image. Production logos are SVG renditions with a custom botanical drawing and outlined Cormorant Garamond lettering. Publication is through the user's GitHub Pages repository
